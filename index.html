    Where do I find my statements?
    1. Open Etsy on a computer and go to Shop Manager.
    2. Choose Finances, then Monthly statements.
    3. Pick a month and download its CSV. Repeat for each month you want to see.
    4. Load all the files here at once. Months are combined, and a file added twice is only counted once.

    The full report is unlocked in this browser.

    Not affiliated with Etsy. Figures come from your own statement files and are a guide, not tax advice. Sales tax and VAT that Etsy collects from buyers are left out, because that money was never yours.
